AutoFrame Quick Guide

One fixed camera. The AI finds the preacher and moves a virtual camera inside the picture to keep them framed, for a normal 16:9 stream and a 9:16 vertical one. Everything runs on your PC; nothing is uploaded.

Set it up once

1

Install the plugin

  1. Close OBS Studio (version 30 or newer).
  2. Run VMedia-AI-AutoFrame-Setup-x64.exe and click Yes on the Windows admin prompt.
  3. Start OBS. It finds the plugin by itself; nothing inside the OBS folder is changed.
2

Add the filter to your camera

  1. Right-click your camera or capture-card source → Filters.
  2. Under Effect Filters click + → V Media AI AutoFrame.
  3. Wait a second for Ready in the status line.

This Filters window is your monitor: it shows the tracking box, the person number and the live stats.

3

Pick a framing preset

In General → Framing preset. It fills in sensible framing, zoom and movement values that you can fine-tune later.

Church PreacherWaist up, slow and calm, holds still at the pulpit.
Worship LeaderIgnores raised hands so the shot doesn't bounce.
GroupFrames the whole team, or the lead plus whoever fits.
Medium ShotTeachers, speakers, interviews.
4

Show it where the stage is

  1. Subject Tracking → Stage area bottom edge: drag it up until it sits just below the stage floor. Only people whose feet stand inside the box are followed, so heads in the front rows never steal the shot.
  2. Ask the preacher to stand at the pulpit, then press Set pulpit to the preacher's current position. While they stay there, the shot stays perfectly still.
  3. Turn on Debug → Show dead zone, pulpit zone and stage area to see the boxes in the Filters window while you adjust.
5

Choose landscape or vertical

Landscape 16:9: keep Output aspect ratio: Same as camera.

Vertical 1080×1920 for YouTube Shorts, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok live:

  1. OBS Settings → Video: Base and Output resolution 1080x1920.
  2. In the filter: Output aspect ratio: 9:16, Output resolution: Auto. The camera then fills the canvas exactly.
  3. Detail sharpening 20-40% helps a 1080p camera; a 4K camera gives the sharpest vertical picture.

Both at once? Keep the camera itself clean, put it in two scenes, and add one AutoFrame filter to each scene: 16:9 on one, 9:16 on the other.

Preacher, 9:16
Worship team, 9:16 · lead singer kept whole
6

Go live. Viewers never see the tracking boxes.

On air: the main preview is exactly what the stream and recording get. Clean picture, no boxes, no text.

For you: the Filters window (or Studio Mode's preview, or a source projector) shows the boxes and stats.

During the service

Assign these in OBS Settings → Hotkeys (listed under the filter's name) or a Stream Deck. Every change is a smooth move, never a jump.

If the preacher steps away

The shot holds for 2.5 s, then glides to a safe wide shot, and picks them up again when they return.

Computer working hard?

Pick Processing mode: Low CPU or tick Use GPU acceleration (works on GTX 1650/1660, no RTX needed).

Frame the camera wide

Digital zoom can only crop. Leave room around everywhere the preacher walks; AutoFrame does the rest.
