V Media AI AutoFrame - by V Media Production

AI auto-framing for OBS Studio: keeps a preacher, presenter or performer framed with smooth
digital pan, tilt and zoom - no PTZ camera needed.

Requirements
  - Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  - OBS Studio 30 or newer (tested with OBS Studio 33.0.1)
  - Any modern CPU. Optional GPU acceleration on any DirectX 12 graphics card
    (GTX 1650 / 1660 and newer - no RTX, CUDA or TensorRT required)

Before you continue
  - Close OBS Studio.

After installing
  1. Start OBS Studio.
  2. Right-click your camera / capture card source > Filters.
  3. Click + under "Effect Filters" and choose "V Media AI AutoFrame".
  4. Pick a Framing preset (for example "Church Preacher Mode").

Privacy
  All video is processed on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, no internet connection is
  used, no account or subscription is needed, and there is no face recognition.
